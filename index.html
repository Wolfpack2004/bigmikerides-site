Meet the driver & owner

This is Mike. He's the one who'll pick you up.

Big Mike Rides isn't a call center dispatching strangers — it's Mike, behind the wheel, who started this company because he was tired of waiting on apps that couldn't tell him who was actually coming. He answers his own phone, remembers his regulars, and treats every ride like he's picking up a neighbor. Because he is.

Wherever you need to go, Big Mike's headed that way.

Four kinds of rides cover almost everything our regulars call about. If yours isn't listed, call anyway — chances are we can still work it out.

Airport transportation

Flat-rate rides to and from DFW and Love Field. Mike tracks your flight, so a delay or an early landing doesn't throw off your pickup.

Everyday rides

Appointments, errands, a ride to work when your car's in the shop — the kind of trip you'd rather not think twice about.

Special occasions & events

Weddings, nights out, graduations — book ahead and everyone gets picked up and dropped off together, no one driving.

Scheduled & recurring rides

Same pickup, same time, every week — dialysis, school runs, standing appointments. Set it up once and Mike remembers.
