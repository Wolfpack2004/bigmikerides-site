Good to know

Questions folks ask before they call.

What areas does Big Mike Rides serve?

Denton County and Collin County, plus the greater Dallas-Fort Worth area. If you're not sure your address is in range, just call or text (945) 544-0098 and ask.

Is Big Mike available 24/7?

Yes. Big Mike answers his own phone any hour, day or night. Late-night airport landings and early-morning pickups are normal for him.

Does Big Mike Rides go to DFW Airport?

Yes, airport runs are one of the most common rides Big Mike does. Just give him your flight number when you book and he'll track it for delays.

How is this different from Uber or Lyft?

Big Mike Rides isn't an app dispatching a random driver. It's Big Mike, personally, in his own clean Jeep Cherokee, every time. Same driver, same reliable vehicle, no surge pricing surprises.

How do I book a ride?

Call, text, or book online. Give Big Mike your pickup, drop-off, and time, and it goes straight on his calendar.

Can I set up a recurring ride?

Yes. Big Mike handles scheduled and recurring pickups for regular commutes, appointments, and routines, not just one-off trips.
